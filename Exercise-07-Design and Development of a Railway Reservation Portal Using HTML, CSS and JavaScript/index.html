Help & Information

Some instructions to use the RailDesk portal.

How to search for trains?

  1. Enter your departure station.
  2. Enter your destination station.
  3. Select your journey date.
  4. Choose your preferred travel class.
  5. Click Search Trains.
  6. Choose a train and enter passenger details.

How to save a booking?

  1. Search for a train.
  2. Click the Book button.
  3. Enter the passenger name and age.
  4. Confirm your reservation.
  5. View the saved ticket under My Bookings.
